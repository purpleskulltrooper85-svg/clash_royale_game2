How to Play

  • Drag cards from your hand onto the battlefield to deploy troops.
  • You can only deploy on your side (bottom) — or in enemy lane territory after you destroy a Princess Tower.
  • Spells (Fireball, Poison) can be dropped anywhere.
  • Elixir refills over time. At 1:00 left, elixir flow doubles!
  • Destroy more towers than the enemy before time runs out. King Tower = instant win.
